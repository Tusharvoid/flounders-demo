Our story

A Newquay favourite since 2008

Flounders has been frying on East Street for over fifteen years — a small, family-run shop with purple paper, a fish on the sign, and a queue of regulars who wouldn’t go anywhere else.

We’re a short stroll from the beaches, so most boxes don’t make it past the harbour wall. But there’s a cosy dining room if you’d rather sit in and take your time.
